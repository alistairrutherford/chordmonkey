# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-18)

The first version published as a GitHub release, as a universal macOS zip (signed ad hoc, not
notarized).

### Added

- **Info View:** a strip along the bottom of the editor, like Live's own Info View. Point at any
  control, chord pad, effects card or the workspace and it shows what it does and what its
  settings mean; elsewhere, a short hint. Heard, Export, Detect and the pattern's Drag MIDI show
  what they hold now. It replaces the tooltips.
- The editor is 64 px taller to make room: 780 × 784 by default, 720 × 744 at its smallest.
- The README's screenshot shows the editor with the Info View.

## 1.0.0

The first version: a scale's chords, chord sets, patterns that play in time with Live, Bind and Keys-Lock, Detect, substitutes and drag-out to MIDI clips. Not published as a release.
